Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This application ("Mail Assistant") accesses your Gmail account solely to send and receive email on your behalf. It does not:

  • Store your email content or metadata on any external server beyond what is necessary for real-time email operations.
  • Share your data with any third party.
  • Use your data for any purpose other than providing email functionality to you.
  • Collect analytics, tracking information, or personal data beyond the OAuth token required for authentication.

All email operations are performed locally on your device. OAuth tokens are stored locally and used only to authenticate with Google's IMAP and SMTP servers.

If you have questions about this policy, contact haojasmine@gmail.com.
